**Primary insight:** Science treats draws from a calibration generator as exchangeable with the unobserved remainder. When the remainder is generated by a different process, more n on the calibration sample cannot identify the target.

**Primary (engine):** 3I/ATLAS isotopic chemistry — Cordiner et al., *Nature*, 22 Jun 2026, doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10771-6. Water D/H = (0.98 ± 0.06)%, 15σ above the Solar System comet mean of 0.029%. Unlike any Solar System body. More local comets will not identify interstellar ice.

**Co-primary (life):** Heimdallarchaeia in oxic coastal sediments — Appler et al., *Nature* 652:405–415, 18 Feb 2026. The anoxic first-catalogue was the wrong generator for the eukaryotic ancestor's environment. Ancestor-was-aerobic remains unproved.
